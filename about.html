About Faders

Built by people who never wanted to leave the front row.

Faders started as a small crew booking backyard shows for local bands around Kampala. These days we run full festival stages, handle booking, production and ticketing for venues and promoters across the city — but the rule hasn't changed: the sound has to hit right, and the room has to feel alive.

From intimate acoustic sundowners to multi-stage block parties, we handle the parts most people never see — sound, lighting, logistics, permits, talent — so artists and audiences only have to show up and feel the show.
